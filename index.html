STATION MASTER 3D

Chandanpur
Junction

You run the station. Every train waits for your signal.

Trains arrive from both sides on four main tracks, A to D, that run into the middle of the station. They stop at the Outer and Home signals until you clear a route. The station has 5 platforms and 3 loop lines, a goods running line and a yard.

You set every route by hand: throw the points (tap a crossover on the panel, or the Route buttons on a train's card) so the path weaves around stabled rakes and waiting trains, then take the signal OFF. Trains follow the points exactly as they lie, curving smoothly from track to track. Wrong points mean run-throughs, wrong platforms or accidents.

  • The dashed line on the panel shows where each train will go with the points as set
  • 🛤 Route on a train's card sets the whole route in one go (points + every signal) to the platform or line chosen on the card; 🛤 Depart does the same for leaving; ✖ cancels
  • Tap A–D at the edges of the panel to keep a main track for arrivals only, departures only, or both
  • Left-drag to rotate · Right-drag to pan · Scroll to zoom
  • Use the camera buttons to sit in the SM cabin or ride in the loco cab
